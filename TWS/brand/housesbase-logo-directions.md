# HousesBase Logo — Final-Round Exploration

## Brand premise

HousesBase is the operating system for a software house: projects, people,
clients, time, payroll, and finance brought into one visible system. The logo
should therefore feel architectural without looking like a real-estate brand,
and systematic without becoming another generic SaaS cube.

Core idea: **One house. One system.**

## Direction review

| No. | Direction | Primary hierarchy | Strategic strength | Watch-out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Foundation H | Symbol-led | Closest evolution of the current column-and-footing mark; stable and grounded | Needs optical refinement so the H reads before the U |
| 02 | Connected Rooms | Symbol-led | Four business systems joined around one operating core | Modular-grid marks are common in SaaS |
| 03 | Operating Grid | Monogram-led | Strong HB logic and a precise software-system character | Internal intersections need simplification at favicon size |
| 04 | Open Doorway | Symbol-led | Best expression of one entry into the whole operation; architectural H without a roof cliché | Accent door must also work in a one-color version |
| 05 | Layered Base | Emblem-led | Communicates multiple operational layers resolving into one base | Dimensional form is less timeless and harder to reproduce |
| 06 | Keystone | Symbol-led | Expresses the central platform holding departments together | Can drift toward institutional or construction branding |
| 07 | House Pulse | Data-led | Connects the house structure to live operating intelligence | Upward-line motif is familiar in analytics brands |
| 08 | Unified Loop | Flow-led | One continuous connected workflow; compact and memorable | Too complex for very small sizes without a simplified micro-mark |
| 09 | North Star Base | Emblem-led | Direction, clarity, and operational control | Compass geometry can suggest navigation or real estate |
| 10 | Wordmark Cut | Wordmark-led | Most distinctive verbal presence; ideal for marketing headers | Companion monogram needs clearer HB legibility |

## Recommendation

Advance **04 Open Doorway** as the primary direction, with the typographic
confidence of **10 Wordmark Cut** and the construction discipline of **03
Operating Grid**.

Recommended production hierarchy:

1. Primary horizontal lockup — doorway H + HousesBase wordmark.
2. Compact lockup — doorway H above the wordmark.
3. Product mark — doorway H alone in navy or white.
4. Micro mark — simplified one-color H for 16–24 px use.
5. Descriptor lockup — `SOFTWARE HOUSE OS` only where the available width is
   at least 180 px.

## Proposed core palette

- Architectural navy: `#17324D`
- Near black: `#101820`
- Warm ivory: `#F5F1E8`
- Terracotta accent: `#C84A32`

## Production acceptance criteria

- Readable at 16 px and recognizable in silhouette.
- Works in pure black, pure white, and a single spot color.
- No dependency on gradients, shadows, or internal micro-detail.
- Separate symbol, horizontal lockup, stacked lockup, and favicon SVG files.
- Clear space based on the doorway's internal opening width.
- Trademark similarity review before launch.
